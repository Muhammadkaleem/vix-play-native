Privacy Policy

Vix Play: All Format Player (com.devbytes.vixplayer.app) · Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information Vix Play ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. Vix Play is a media player: your videos, music and playback history stay on your device. The app is free and supported by advertising, so some device information is processed by our advertising and app-quality partners, as described below.

1. Information that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your device. We do not receive it and it is never uploaded by the app:

  • Your media files: videos and audio the app finds through Android's media permissions, or files you open with Vix Play.
  • Playback data: resume positions, watch history, favourites, playlists, pinned folders, equalizer presets and app settings.
  • Network streams and shares: stream URLs you enter and saved network-share (SMB/FTP) server details. Saved passwords are stored encrypted on your device.
  • Calendar events: only if you turn on Daily Briefing and grant calendar access. Today's events are read on your device to build your briefing and are not sent anywhere.
  • Pro rewards: the Pro time you earn by watching rewarded ads is recorded on your device.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of this.

2. Information processed by third-party services

Vix Play uses the following Google services. They may collect device information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating-system version, app version and app interaction data, under their own privacy policies.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not have accounts, logins or our own servers that store your data.

3. Advertising and your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in these regions, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy options in the app or by clearing the app's data.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices).
  • Ad-free time: watching an optional rewarded ad gives you temporary ad-free time ("Pro"). All other features are free with or without Pro.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

4. Permissions the app requests

  • Photos and videos / Music and audio (or Storage on older Android): to find and play media on your device.
  • Notifications: for playback controls, Daily Briefing and optional reminders.
  • Calendar (optional): only for Daily Briefing.
  • Internet and network state: for network streaming, ads, crash reporting and analytics.
  • Foreground service (media playback): to keep audio playing when you leave the app.
  • Alarms and run at startup: to deliver the Daily Briefing at the time you choose.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob as described above.

5. Data retention and deletion

On-device data is kept until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Data held by Google services is kept according to Google's retention policies. Firebase Analytics data is retained for a limited period, after which it is deleted automatically.

6. Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. Saved network-share passwords are encrypted on your device.

7. Children

Vix Play is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold your data on our own servers, most requests are handled by changing your consent choice, resetting your advertising ID, or clearing the app's data. For anything else, contact us below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "Last updated" date above will change, and significant changes will be highlighted in the app or its store listing.
